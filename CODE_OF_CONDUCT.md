# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must help keep this project welcoming, constructive, and safe.
Expected behavior includes technical curiosity, respectful disagreement, actionable feedback,
and consideration for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, publication of another person's private information, and personal attacks are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It
also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
consistent, and focused on protecting the community.

Conduct concerns that can safely be discussed in public may be raised in a repository issue. Do
not publish private or identifying information. For confidential platform-level abuse reports,
use [GitHub's reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Maintainers will
respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as the available channel permits.
